## Assumptions and decisions

- **as_connection = BigQuery**; connection database `atscale-sales-demo`, schema `BANK_ENTERPRISE_PERFORMANCE` — from build.yaml and the DDL qualifier.
- **Date conformed across all four facts (Rule 21)** — each fact has one event date (snapshot_date, origination_date, activity_month), joined to the same plain Date Dimension at Day; no role-play.
- **Snapshot balances are semi-additive (last non-empty over Date)** — EOM Balance, Average Daily Balance, Deposit Account Count, Commitment Amount, Outstanding Balance, Undrawn Amount, Nonperforming Balance use `sum` + `semi_additive: last` on their fact's date relationship. Snapshots are dated the 1st of each month, so a quarter or year reports its last monthly snapshot (Q4 = December 1st), added across branches/products/facilities but never across months.
- **NIM averaged** — Net Interest Margin (bps) uses `average`, per "average it, never add it"; Average Interest Rate and Average LTV also use `average`.
- **Ratios computed from totals** — NPL Ratio, 90+ DPD Rate, Any-DPD Rate and Average Loan Size are `metric_calc`s using `Divide` over base totals (NPL Ratio over the two semi-additive balances, so both come from the latest snapshot).
- **Year-over-year / YTD** — the metrics the catalog flags (EOM Balance, Outstanding Balance, Loan Amount Originated, Fee Revenue) get Prior Year (`ParallelPeriod` at Year) and YoY Growth % calcs; Fee Revenue also gets Year to Date (`PeriodsToDate` at Year). Quarter and Month carry `parallel_periods` keyed on the same level one year earlier (`[prior_year_num, quarter_num]`, `[prior_year_num, month_num]`), with `prior_year_num = year_num - 1` added to dim_date.
- **Filtered counts and balances use calculated columns** — `is_30_dpd`, `is_60_dpd`, `is_90_plus_dpd`, `is_delinquent` on fact_loan_origination and `nonperforming_balance` on fact_loan_facility_snapshot implement the metrics' WHERE clauses; the flag counts are summed.
- **Branch geography from Market (Rule 5)** — Region > State > Market from dim_market, Branch from dim_branch, joined by an intra-dimension snowflake on market_id. dim_branch.state/region and the facts' denormalized market_id are not used, per their Metabase descriptions.
- **Line of Business** — the Line of Business Dimension (Bank Segment > Line of Business) joins only the facility fact, which carries lob_id. Other facts roll up by line of business through the Product dimension's Product Line of Business level. The dim_product.lob_id FK is not modeled as a second path, to avoid two routes to the same dimension from the facility fact.
- **Degenerate dimensions** — Deposit Customer Segment (deposit fact), Delinquency Status, FICO Band and Vintage (origination fact) are fact-resident, `is_degenerate: true`, listed in the model `dimensions:` block.
- **is_unique_key** — set only where the data profile shows distinct = rows: Day, Market, Branch, Customer, Product, Line of Business, Rating Grade.
- **Data types (Rule 22)** — BigQuery NUMERIC (no precision) → `decimal`, FLOAT → `double`, INTEGER → `long`, DATE → `date`, BOOLEAN → `boolean`.
- **Calendar sort** — Quarter sorts by `quarter_sort = year_num * 10 + quarter_num` (calculated column), so quarters order chronologically rather than alphabetically.
- **Metric folders** follow the Metabase sub-collections (line of business).
