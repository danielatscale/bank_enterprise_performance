business catalog read out of Metabase (database `Bank Enterprise Performance`, collection
`Bank Enterprise Performance - Business Catalog`). Nothing in it was copied from any other SML model.

## Assumptions and decisions

- **Snapshot facts**: `fact_deposit_snapshot` and `fact_loan_facility_snapshot` balances (EOM Balance, Average Daily Balance, Deposit Account Count, Commitment, Outstanding, Undrawn, Nonperforming Balance) are `sum` + `semi_additive: last` over their date relationship, so a quarter or year reports its latest monthly snapshot (dated the 1st) and never adds months. Flows (accounts opened/closed, interest, fees, volumes, originations) are plain sums.
- **Conformed date (Rule 21)**: each fact has one event date (snapshot_date, origination_date, activity_month), so all four join the same Date Dimension at Day with no role-play.
- **Year-over-year (Rule 13)**: the calendar has partial years (Jul-Dec 2023, Jan-Jun 2026), so default ParallelPeriod (relative child position) would mis-align, e.g. Q1 2024 with Q3 2023. Following the Internet Sales `Custom PP445` pattern, Quarter, Month and Day each carry `parallel_periods` keyed on the same level one year earlier (`prior_year_quarter_key`, `prior_year_month`, `prior_year_date`, calculated columns on `dim_date`) against the Year level. This replaces Rule 13's generic `prior_year_num` column, which keys the Year level rather than the shifted level.
- **YoY / YTD coverage**: Prior Year and YoY Growth % for the metrics whose descriptions ask for it (EOM Balance, Loan Amount Originated, Outstanding Balance, Fee Revenue), plus Fee Revenue YTD (PeriodsToDate on Year).
- **Ratios from totals**: Any-DPD Rate, 90+ DPD Rate, Average Loan Size and NPL Ratio are `metric_calc` divisions of summed components, so they are never averaged. Net Interest Margin (bps) is `average` with no semi-additive block (Rule 19).
- **Filtered counts**: delinquency counts and Nonperforming Balance use calculated columns on their fact datasets (`is_delinquent`, `is_30_dpd`, `is_60_dpd`, `is_90_plus_dpd`, `nonperforming_balance`).
- **Quarter key**: `quarter_name` ('Q1 2025') sorts alphabetically, so Quarter is keyed and sorted on a calculated `quarter_key` = year * 10 + quarter, with `quarter_name` as the display name.
- **Branch geography**: per the Branch table description, Region and State come from `dim_market` (snowflake `dim_branch.market_id` -> Market), not the denormalized branch columns. The fact `market_id` copies are not joined.
- **Line of business**: the Line of Business dimension joins only the facility snapshot (the only fact with `lob_id`). Every fact also rolls up by Product Line of Business through the Product dimension.
- **Customer hierarchies**: Customer Location and Industry Vertical hierarchies end in the hidden Customer level so each has a path to the facts (Rule 23).
- **Degenerate dimensions**: Deposit Customer Segment, Delinquency Status, FICO Band and Vintage live on their fact tables (`is_degenerate: true`, listed in the model `dimensions:` block).
- **is_unique_key (Rule 16)**: set only where the key column's exact distinct count equals the table row count in `context/data_profile.yaml` (Day, Market, Branch, Customer, Product, Line of Business, Rating Grade).
- **data_type (Rule 22)**: BigQuery NUMERIC (no precision stated) -> `decimal`, FLOAT -> `double`, INTEGER -> `long`.
- **Connection**: `as_connection: BigQuery`, the BigQuery connection name on the AtScale server.
- **Excluded columns**: `dim_branch.state`/`region` and the facts' `market_id` are kept on their datasets but not modeled as levels, per their Metabase descriptions ("Prefer Market...", "Do not join on it").

## Generation summary

- Datasets: 11 (7 dimension tables, 4 fact tables)
- Dimensions: 10 (Date [time], Branch, Customer, Product, Line of Business, Risk Rating, plus 4 degenerate)
- Base metrics: 26 (7 semi-additive last-non-empty)
- Calculated metrics: 13
- Model relationships: 15
- Role-play prefixes: none (conformed Date Dimension)
- Snowflake bridges: Branch Dimension (`dim_branch.market_id` -> Market)
- Calculated columns: dim_date (4), fact_loan_origination (4), fact_loan_facility_snapshot (1)
- Caveat: Consumer Lending delinquency and current balance are as of June 2026, not a monthly history, so slicing them by origination date shows the current status of loans booked in that period.
